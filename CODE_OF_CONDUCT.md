# Code of conduct

Tessera is maintained by people who want a welcoming, useful project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other project space) is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent, especially toward newcomers;
- give and take feedback on the work, not the person;
- accept that maintainers decide what fits the project, and explain decisions when asked;
- keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexual content, doxxing or
  threats.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its full code of conduct.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour with GitHub's **Report content** option and open an issue titled "Code of conduct report" (no details) mentioning @vvipinthomas; the maintainer will reply with a private way to share them. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who break this code, following
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
