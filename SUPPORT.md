# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Start with the [user guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.md) and the [FAQ](docs/USER_GUIDE.md#10-troubleshooting-and-faq), then ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/vvipinthomas/tessera/discussions).
- **Something is broken.** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/vvipinthomas/tessera/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with your version (`tessera version`) and steps to reproduce.
- **Security issue.** Do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Deployment questions.** See [docs/DEPLOY.md](docs/DEPLOY.md) and [docs/CONFIGURATION.md](docs/CONFIGURATION.md).

Tessera is maintained by volunteers. There is no paid support today.
