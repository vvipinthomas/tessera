# Governance

Tessera is maintained by **Vipin Thomas** ([@vvipinthomas](https://github.com/vvipinthomas)), who makes final decisions
on scope, design and releases.

- **Decisions** are discussed in issues and pull requests. Larger changes start as an issue labelled `proposal` and stay
  open for at least a week for comments.
- **Becoming a maintainer:** contributors with a track record of good reviews and merged work over several months may
  be invited to become maintainers with merge rights. Maintainers are listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`.
- **Releases** follow semantic versioning. Security fixes are released as soon as they are ready.
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0. Contributions are accepted under the same licence with a DCO sign-off; no CLA.

This document will grow as the community does.
